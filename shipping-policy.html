Shipping Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

At Yatri Luggege, managed by Sole Proprietor Ravi Singh Dhakre, we understand how important it is for your travel gear to arrive quickly and in pristine condition. We partner with the most reliable logistics operators in India to ensure a seamless delivery experience. This Shipping Policy details our guidelines, processing timelines, transit procedures, and customer responsibilities.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines

All successful orders placed on our website are processed within 24 to 48 hours (excluding Sundays and national holidays). Once your order is packaged and handpicked by our team, it is handed over to our logistics partner. You will receive an immediate notification containing your dispatch status and tracking details.

2. Domestic Estimated Delivery Timelines

We deliver to virtually all pin codes across India. The estimated transit time depends on your geographical location:

  • Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune): 3 to 5 business days post-dispatch.
  • State Capitals & Major Towns: 4 to 6 business days post-dispatch.
  • Tier-2, Tier-3 Cities & Semi-Urban Areas: 5 to 7 business days post-dispatch.
  • Northeast India, Jammu & Kashmir, and Remote Regions: 7 to 10 business days post-dispatch.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping fees are calculated dynamically at checkout based on package weight, dimensions, and the delivery destination. From time to time, we may offer promotional free shipping on select product categories or order amounts, which will be clearly indicated on the checkout page.

4. Delivery Coverage & Logistics Partners

We offer comprehensive delivery coverage across India. To ensure safety and promptness, we partner with premier third-party courier services, including BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, ExpressBees, and Xpressbees. Please note that we do not deliver to P.O. boxes.

5. Real-Time Consignment Tracking

As soon as your shipment is dispatched from our sorting facility, you will receive a tracking link via email and/or SMS. You can use this link to check the real-time status of your package. Please allow up to 24 hours for the tracking information to update on the courier portal.

6. Unforeseen Delivery Delays

While we strive to meet all estimated timelines, occasionally delays may occur due to circumstances beyond our control (such as extreme weather conditions, national strikes, natural disasters, local security lockdowns, or logistical bottlenecks at the courier end). We appreciate your patience during these rare events and will actively coordinate with the logistics team to expedite delivery.

7. Customer Responsibility: Accurate Delivery Address

It is the customer's sole responsibility to ensure that the complete, accurate delivery address—including the flat/house number, street name, landmark, city, state, and exact 6-digit pin code—along with a working mobile number is provided at the time of purchase. Yatri Luggege is not responsible for misdeliveries or package returns resulting from incorrect or incomplete addresses supplied by the user.

8. Non-Delivery and Package Returns (RTO)

Our courier partners will attempt delivery up to 3 times before returning the package to our warehouse (Return to Origin). If a package is returned due to customer unavailability, refusal to accept, or an invalid address, a re-shipping fee will be charged to dispatch the consignment again.

9. Crucial Transit Damage & Unboxing Video Policy

We ensure that all suitcases, trolley bags, and backpacks are packaged with high-grade, bubble-wrap layers and robust cardboard boxes to withstand transit friction. However, in the rare event that your product arrives damaged, you must:

  • Record a complete, continuous unboxing video from the moment you start opening the outer seal/box to the inspection of the product. The video must be uninterrupted and clearly show the damage.
  • Report the transit damage to us via email within 24 hours of delivery. Claims made after 24 hours or without a valid unboxing video will not be approved under any circumstances due to courier guidelines.

10. Proof of Delivery (POD)

Deliveries may require an OTP (One-Time Password) confirmation sent to your registered mobile number, or a physical signature upon receipt. Once the package status is updated to "Delivered" on the courier network, it is deemed successfully handed over. Any claims regarding non-receipt of packages marked as delivered must be reported within 24 hours.

11. International Shipping Disclaimer

Currently, Yatri Luggege only caters to orders within the domestic boundaries of India. We do not support international checkout or shipping to overseas locations.
