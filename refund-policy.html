Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

At Yatri Luggege, represented by Sole Proprietor Ravi Singh Dhakre, we strive to build deep relationships with our customers based on trust and quality. We take immense care in designing and manufacturing high-grade suitcases, trolleys, and travel bags. In case you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, our Refund Policy outlines a fair, transparent, and structured process for returns, replacements, and refunds.

1. Strict 7-Day Return Window

We offer a strict 7-day return and exchange policy. This means you have exactly 7 calendar days from the date of package delivery (as registered by the logistics company) to submit your request for a return or replacement. Return requests initiated after 7 days will be automatically rejected under all circumstances.

2. Permitted Reasons for Returns & Exchanges

To avoid misuse, returns or exchanges are only processed under the following specific conditions:

  • The product received has a structural manufacturing defect (e.g. faulty trolley handle mechanism, non-functional wheels, broken zipper track).
  • The product arrived physically damaged due to transit.
  • The product delivered does not match the order details (e.g., incorrect model, color, or size).

3. Mandatory Unboxing Video Requirement

Because we perform multi-stage quality checks before sealing every order, we require concrete proof of damage or inaccuracies. **You must record a single, continuous, uninterrupted unboxing video** starting from the sealed outer shipping box showing the label details, through the process of opening, up to the identification of the defect. We reserve the absolute right to refuse returns if this video proof is missing or clearly edited.

4. Return Eligibility & Product Condition

To qualify for a refund or replacement, the returned product must meet the following criteria:

  • It must be completely unused, unwashed, and free of dirt, scuff marks, or physical scratches.
  • It must be housed in its absolute original packaging, including bubble covers, tags, labels, protective layers, and dust bags.
  • All locks, compartments, keychains, and tags must remain completely intact.

5. Return Request Submission Procedure

To initiate a return or replacement request, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an official email to yatriluggege06@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line format: **Return Request - Order #[Your Order Number]**.
  3. Attach your unboxing video, detailed photographs of the defect/damage, and a copy of the digital invoice.
  4. Our administrative desk will review the submission within 24 to 48 hours and provide further instructions.

6. Logistics & Return Shipping Fees

In cases where the return is due to our error (defective or wrong product sent), we will arrange a complimentary reverse-pickup from your delivery address. If a reverse-pickup service is unavailable in your pin code, we will request you to self-ship the item via a local courier and reimburse standard shipping costs against a valid receipt. For voluntary returns or exchanges where no defect exists, the customer shall bear the actual shipping costs.

7. Quality Inspection Process at Warehouse

Once the returned product arrives back at our registered sorting facility, it undergoes a meticulous quality inspection by our assessment team. This process is completed within **48 hours** of package receipt. We will notify you via email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund or exchange request based on product condition.

8. Approved Refund Timelines & Methods

If your return is officially approved, the refund will be processed immediately. The refund will be credited back to your **original payment method** (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking, or wallet) via our payment gateway partner (PhonePe). Under standard banking cycles, it takes **5 to 10 business days** for the credited amount to reflect in your account statement.

9. Order Cancellation & Refunds

You can cancel an order only before it has been dispatched from our facility. Prepaid orders canceled before shipment are eligible for a **100% full refund** credited to the original source. Once the consignment has been handed over to the shipping partner, cancellations are not possible, and the standard return policy will apply.

10. Exchange Option (Product Swapping)

Instead of a refund, you may choose to exchange the product for a different color, size, or model. If the exchanged item has a higher price, you must pay the difference prior to shipping. If the price is lower, we will issue a partial refund of the difference to your account once the original item is safely returned and inspected.

11. Dealing with Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days, we suggest checking your bank account statement again, then contacting your credit card company or banking institution, as there is often a processing delay. If you have done this and still have not received the refund, please contact us immediately.
